# 08 — End-User Guide (Host & Guest)

## 8.1 How to Start a Meeting as a Host
1. Open the application homepage (`https://teams.yourdomain.com/`).
2. Click **Start Instant Meeting** directly on the hero banner, OR click **Sign In** in the top navigation to access your host workspace.
3. In the Host Dashboard, you can optionally type a custom meeting topic (e.g. `Weekly Sync`) and click **New Meeting**.
4. You will be taken to the **Pre-Join Lobby**.
5. Click **Join Meeting** to enter the live room.
6. Click **Invite Link** on the top right toolbar to copy the shareable meeting link to your clipboard, and paste it into Slack, WhatsApp, or an email to your attendees.

## 8.2 How to Join a Meeting as a Guest
1. Click the meeting link sent to you by the host (e.g. `https://teams.yourdomain.com/room/abc-defg-hij/lobby/`).
2. Your browser will ask for **Camera and Microphone permissions**. Click **Allow**.
3. You will see your live camera feed. Speak into your microphone and confirm the green audio meter pulses.
4. Enter your name into the **Your Display Name** field (e.g. `Sarah Jenkins`).
5. Click the blue **Join Meeting** button. You are now inside the live video call!

## 8.3 Using Meeting Controls During a Call
The floating toolbar at the bottom of the screen provides easy-to-access controls:

| Icon / Control | Action | Keyboard Shortcut |
|---|---|---|
| **Microphone Button** | Mute or unmute your voice. Turns red when muted. | `M` |
| **Camera Button** | Turn your webcam video feed on or off. | `V` |
| **Share Screen Button** | Share your whole screen, a specific window, or a browser tab. | `S` |
| **Low-Bandwidth Mode** | Disables incoming/outgoing video to conserve bandwidth on weak Wi-Fi/mobile data. | — |
| **Record Meeting Button**| Starts in-browser HD call recording. Shows a pulsing red `REC` badge. Click again to stop and download the `.webm` file. | — |
| **Chat Button** | Opens the slide-out text chat drawer to send notes or links. | — |
| **Participants Button** | Opens the attendee drawer showing who is currently in the room. | — |
| **Leave Button** | Exits the call and returns you to the summary page. | — |
